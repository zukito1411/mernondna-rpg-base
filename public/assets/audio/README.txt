Mernodna audio sources

Footsteps, sword swishes, heavy slams, metallic skill impacts, and soft-hit
OGG effects are selected from Kenney's RPG Audio and Impact Sounds packs.
Both packs are CC0 1.0 Universal:

RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The skill-specific samples are `sword-whoosh.ogg` (RPG Audio, `drawKnife2`),
`heavy-slam.ogg` (Impact Sounds, `impactSoft_heavy_001`), and
`energy-impact.ogg` (Impact Sounds, `impactMetal_light_001`).
