Mernodna audio sources

Footsteps, heavy slams, metallic skill impacts, and heavy hit OGG effects are
selected from Kenney's RPG Audio and Impact Sounds packs.
Both packs are CC0 1.0 Universal:

RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The skill-specific samples are `sword-whoosh.ogg` (RPG Audio, `drawKnife2`),
`heavy-slam.ogg` (Impact Sounds, `impactSoft_heavy_001`), `impactPunch_heavy_000.ogg`
and `impactPunch_heavy_003.ogg` (Impact Sounds), and
`energy-impact.ogg` (Impact Sounds, `impactMetal_light_001`).
The sword attack slash is "Sword slash swoosh" from Mixkit:
https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/sword/
Mixkit Sound Effects Free License: https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree
Enemy damage uses "Sword cutting flesh" by itself, from the Mixkit Sword
sound effects collection and its Free License.

The background score is "The Britons" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Listen/download: https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN2600004

The environmental weather ambience is synthesized by the game at runtime.
The background score is a licensed recording, not a synthesized score, and
does not reproduce an existing film or game soundtrack.

Regional background music
-------------------------

The score changes with the current world region and crossfades between tracks.
No new music is synthesized by the game.

  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Balletto_detto_Il_conte_Orlando.ogg
