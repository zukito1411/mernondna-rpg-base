# Selected side-walking prompt set

Tool: built-in image generation, real transparent alpha. Source masters remain
in this folder; only packed runtime atlases ship with the game.

## Four-pose source and focused correction

Use case: identity-preserve. Original `walk.png` defines the upright compact
side-walking silhouette, curled tail, proportions and folded wings.
`attack.png` and `fly.png` define matching charcoal scales, blood-red membranes,
orange throat plates, gold claws/horns and crisp detailed pixel-RPG rendering.

Draw four complete right-facing walking poses in a transparent 2x2 sheet,
keeping head/torso scale and hip/shoulder height consistent. Both forelegs and
both hindlegs must remain anatomically connected and separately readable.
Use contact, passing, opposite contact and opposite passing. Alternate which
diagonal pair lifts; do not freeze either limb pair to animate the other.
Keep the upright compact stance, not a low sprawling attack stance. Whole
poses only, no stitched limbs, extra legs, clipping, floor, shadows, glow,
labels or background. Preserve clear gutters around all tips and claws.

Focused source correction: reveal the far foreleg and its separately planted
claws in the bottom-left opposite-contact pose rather than fusing both front
legs. Preserve colors, head, wings, tail and complete-body consistency.
The source sheet's bottom-right pose remained unsuitable and is not consumed.

## Final single-pose opposite passing

Use case identity-preserve. Produce ONE complete dragon pose ONLY, not a
sheet. Use the exact dragon from the reference four-frame sheet, same charcoal
detailed pixel scales, dark red folded wings, orange throat and gold claws,
same upright side stance, head/torso proportions, compact curled tail and
slightly elevated right-facing SIDE camera. Same design, not a new dragon.
Complete full body with ample transparent padding, real alpha, no
glow/backdrop/ground/shadow/labels.

This ONE pose is the OPPOSITE WALK PASSING frame, not one of the existing
reference poses. The NEAR-SIDE FRONT LEG (the larger front leg closest to
viewer, attached at the front of chest) must visibly LIFT off the ground,
bend at its ELBOW, and reach its wrist/claws FORWARD to the RIGHT with all
its toes up, around 35px above the planted foot. That lifted near FORELEG
must be the most clearly moving limb, not a planted straight front leg.
The FAR FRONT LEG is separately visible behind it, extended DOWN and PLANTED
to support the chest, its clawed foot distinct below and slightly left of
lifted near-front foot. Two front legs, two separately readable front feet,
never fused.

The NEAR HIND LEG (large rear thigh closest to viewer) is STRAIGHTENED DOWN
and PLANTED under hip supporting body, NOT lifted or folded under belly.
The FAR HIND LEG is visible behind it, with knee/hock bent and foot LIFTED
swinging forward under belly. Thus NEAR FRONT + FAR HIND lift; NEAR HIND +
FAR FRONT plant. Exactly this opposite diagonal. No extra legs. All four
anatomically connected limbs visible, plus two folded wings, no wings
touching floor. No copying the reference top-right's lifted near-hind pose.
Keep torso at same height, only natural limb articulations change; no jump
or lunge.

Single cohesive full dragon, no detached limbs/collage, complete
tail/horns/wings/toes. Same neutral lighting and palette as input. Intended
cell aspect 768x512, dragon occupies about610 wide by430 high with transparent
outer padding.
