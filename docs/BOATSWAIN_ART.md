## Registration and behavior

`src/data/boatswainArt.ts` records all paths, source boot baselines, frame indices and animation sequences. `src/game/systems/BoatswainArt.ts` creates a single 76-frame atlas with a fixed body calibration per facing/state. Scarf registration centers the head without letting the mallet drag the character sideways. Walking calibration comes from neutral poses; combat uses ready/recovered standing poses, and fallen bodies keep that scale. Extra padding beneath the feet holds the lowered mallet; padded frames retain the same collision-foot anchor.

Current walking loops use four full poses: contact, opposite-foot passing, opposite contact, and other-foot passing, at 7fps before movement-speed adjustment. Direction changes retain cycle progress. Left/right walking use one canonical side cycle, mirrored during atlas preparation, so both have identical phase ordering and proportions. The selected side poses avoid the source support pose that switches the mallet hand. Front walking combines the original two opposite contacts with new lifted passing poses; back walking uses four selected new poses. Neutral/idle frames and unused old stride cells never enter walk playback. Each source/facing shares a fixed scale; no torso/leg compositing is used.

Combat artwork and timing are unchanged. The 14-frame attack at 10fps repeats lift/overhead frames until impact at 700ms, then recovers for 700ms. The frontal warning and damage check use the same 82-unit reach. Ordinary stagger immunity lasts 1800ms after the hurt pose, while forced Flurry suppression remains available.

The atlas is 2048x2560 at density two, with 128x128 logical cells and 16 units of foot padding (20 MiB raw RGBA). All three source textures are released after preparation. No measured GPU/device/frame-time claim is made.
