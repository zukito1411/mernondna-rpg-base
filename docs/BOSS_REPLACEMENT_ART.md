## Source layouts and consumption

Walking sheets have 5 columns x 3 rows (down/right/up); combat sheets have 6 columns x 3 rows (windup/impact/recovery/hurt/collapse/fallen). Whole-subject segmentation handles approximate generated spacing instead of blindly clipping rectangular PNG cells. The prepared atlas has 44 cells: four facings of five locomotion poses and six combat poses. Idle uses the upright combat recovery, walking excludes idle. Krag's right-side cycle selects new contact/passing pairs; left mirrors the complete poses. Rootfather's opposing vertical supports mirror complete unarmed poses. Wyrm retains its four-legged source cycle.

Source pairs calibrate scale against their contact/standing pose, not every individual raised knee. New boss foot registration uses the lower silhouette instead of crest/crown/axe position. Complete pose bounds are checked by the production atlas preparation path; a preparation failure reports an error and defers the actor rather than constructing a broken sprite. This is not a claim that rendered registration has been verified.

Attacks hold windup until the existing committed damage time, then show impact/recovery. Hurt and two full collapse/fallen poses use the same upright calibration. Krag uses frontal melee and summons orc raiders. Frost Wyrm keeps its warned ground lunge and summons native frost-wolf packs using approved wolf anatomy/AI, pale tint, alpha roles and existing spacing. Rootfather keeps its radial slam and marsh-wraith summons. No other boss model was regenerated.

All bosses receive a 12% visual, body-radius and attack-footprint increase together. IDs, quests, saved damage fractions, defeat ledgers, respawn timers and cutscene hooks are retained. Existing persistent Krag boar reinforcements are not guessed/mutated into unrelated orcs; new summon calls use orc raiders. Legacy Frostlands gray-wolf records migrate to frost wolves without resetting their IDs, wounds, alpha/group roles or defeated states.

The existing on-demand M14 loader is extended, not duplicated: at most two ambient family atlases and one boss atlas are resident. Bosses load only on nearby content activation, get queue priority and cannot be starved by two live ambient textures. Live references are protected; unused same-category atlases can be evicted after five seconds; source images are released after preparation. Largest new atlas is Rootfather at 3584 x 2688 RGBA (~36.75 MiB); this is a source allocation calculation, not measured GPU/mobile performance.
